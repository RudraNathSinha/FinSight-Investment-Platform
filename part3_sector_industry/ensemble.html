How sector & stock ranks are built

Illustrative offline model — not a forecast and not country ranking.

FinSight Sector & Industry Intelligence ranks business areas and stocks within sectors using a multi-criteria ensemble. Each method scores the same input metrics differently; the ensemble combines them for a stable ordering.

SAW

Simple Additive Weighting — weighted average of normalised metrics. Easy to interpret.

TOPSIS

Ranks names by distance to an ideal best and worst profile in metric space.

VIKOR

Compromise ranking under conflicting criteria; emphasises balance versus extremes.

GRA

Grey Relational Analysis — useful when relationships between metrics are partially known.

WASPAS / COPRAS

Weighted aggregated sum product and complex proportional assessment — alternative multi-criteria formulations.

PCA

Principal components compress correlated metrics into factors before ranking.

How to use this as an analyst

  • Treat ranks as a structured screen, not a buy list.
  • Open a company in Global Equity Universe for live statements and the equity research pack.
  • Do not compare these ranks to Country Ranking scores — different models and units.
