# FinSight — QA paths & 8-minute sales demo

## QA — 10 analyst paths

### Country-first (5)

1. Landing → Open Country Ranking (new window) → confirm banner “Country ranking (not sector or stock ranking)”.
2. Rankings table loads; open top country → score + dimension blocks visible.
3. Click **Explore listings in this country** → Global Equity Universe opens with country filter/search.
4. From that universe, open one company → overview shows provenance; generate equity report.
5. Confirm methodology/disclaimer links work on landing and match legal line.

### Sector-first (5)

6. Landing → Open Sector Intelligence (new window) → coverage cards show sector counts.
7. Open ensemble page → methods explained as illustrative offline model.
8. Open Sectors & Industries → pick Technology → see ranks (not country ranks).
9. Open a stock from sector ranks → Stock Analysis notes sector context (not institutional pack).
10. From hub, user can still open Global Equity Universe separately for full equity research pack.
