Methodology

FinSight is an educational market-intelligence workspace. It organises three separate analytical jobs under one brand. Rank models are never merged.

Three separate models

Country ranking — Compares economies on multi-block indicators (macro, finance, infrastructure, social, and related dimensions). Output is a country score and rank. It does not rank sectors or stocks.
Global equity universe — Organises listings by country, exchange, sector, and industry; supports company analysis and equity research packs. Classification is organisational, not a rank score.
Sector & industry intelligence — MSCI-style sector/industry structure plus offline multi-criteria ranks for sectors and stocks within sectors. These ranks are not country ranks.

Company data preference

Where live company data is requested, FinSight prefers public sources in order: primary scrape where available, yfinance, free public APIs, merged bundle fields, rule-based narrative synthesis, and offline country packs only as last resort when live sources are empty.

Reports

Equity research packs are structured educational documents assembled from available free data. Sensitivity tables, SWOT-style sections, and recommendation labels are illustrative heuristics—not target prices or personalised advice.

Limitations

  • Free data may be incomplete, delayed, or inconsistent across secondary listings.
  • Offline ranks depend on the quality of the underlying workbook inputs.
  • Always reconcile material figures with primary exchange filings.
